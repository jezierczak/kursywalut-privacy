Privacy Policy

Fast Currency Calculator (Polish name: Kursy Walut) · Android app · Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy describes how the app Fast Currency Calculator (package com.finance.jarek.kursywalut, the "app") handles information. The app is published by Jarosław Jezierczak ("I", "developer").

Summary

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. I do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, precise location or any other personal data. The only data that leaves your device is described below and is handled by third parties (exchange-rate providers, Google AdMob and Google Play).

Data stored on your device

Your favourite currencies, base currency, rate alerts, app settings (theme, rate sources) and downloaded exchange rates are stored only locally on your device. I have no access to them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Exchange-rate sources

To show rates the app downloads data from public services: Frankfurter / European Central Bank (api.frankfurter.dev), the National Bank of Poland (api.nbp.pl) and a public currency data set served through jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any internet request, this reveals your IP address to those services. The app sends only the currency codes it needs and nothing that identifies you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use, among other things, your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location may be derived), app interaction data and diagnostic/performance data, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Ads can be removed permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time purchase "Remove ads" is processed by Google Play. I do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase has been made, to restore it after reinstalling or on a new device.

Notifications and widgets

If you create a rate alert, the app periodically checks the rate in the background and shows a notification when your chosen level is reached. Notification permission is optional. Home-screen widgets show the rate of one chosen currency; the widget settings are stored locally.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

I do not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Data processed by Google (ads, purchases) is subject to Google's policies and your rights regarding it can be exercised with Google, including through your Google account's privacy settings. If you are in the EEA/UK you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Information only, not financial advice

Exchange rates in the app are for information purposes only. They may be delayed or differ from the rates offered by banks and exchange offices, and they are not financial advice or an offer to exchange currency.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes (for example, new features), I will update this page and the date above.

Źródła kursów walut

AdMob może zbierać i wykorzystywać m.in. reklamowy identyfikator urządzenia, adres IP (z którego można określić przybliżoną lokalizację), dane o interakcjach z aplikacją oraz dane diagnostyczne i o wydajności, aby wyświetlać i mierzyć reklamy oraz zapobiegać nadużyciom. Reklamowy identyfikator możesz zresetować lub usunąć w ustawieniach Androida (Ustawienia → Prywatność → Reklamy).
